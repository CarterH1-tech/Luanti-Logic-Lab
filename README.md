# Luanti-Logic-Lab
Luanti Logic Lab assignment: 4-bit adder and 1-to-4 demultiplexer
For my attempt at the 4-bit adder:

Because of time restraints and frustration with not being able to understand what I am supposedd to do, I was only able to make a full-bit adder along with a duplicate of it.

It works by having at least two of the switches on or more. If there is any less on the final lightblock will not illuminate.
This is because of the different gates being XOR, AND, along with OR showing which lightstone should be on and which one should be off.
